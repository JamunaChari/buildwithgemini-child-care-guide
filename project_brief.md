# My agent: LittleSteps - Child Care & Milestone Guide

One-liner: A conversational agent that helps parents and caregivers track child development milestones with personalized care steps, age-appropriate activities, and routine management.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Child's name, birthdate/exact age, allergies, dietary restrictions, and logged/achieved milestones.
- Tools: Milestone lookup (fetching age-based pediatric guidelines), recommended care steps finder, and age-appropriate activity suggestions.
- Catalog/UI: Milestone checklists per age tier (e.g. 3 months, 6 months, 12 months), nutrition schedules, and care step cards.
- Image gen: Illustrative visual guides for sensory play, craft ideas, and infant interaction diagrams.
- Sandbox: Exact age calculation in weeks/months, sleep/feeding timing math, and growth percentile calculations.

Core rails (everyone): memory, tools, eval, deploy, frontend
My stretch menu (pick later): A2UI milestone cards & checklists, custom activity image generation, code sandbox for age & growth math
First eval question: What physical and motor milestones should I expect for a 6-month-old, and what daily care steps and activities help support sitting up?
